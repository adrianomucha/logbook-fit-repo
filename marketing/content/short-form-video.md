# Short-Form Video: Instagram Reels + TikTok

3 to 5 per week. Phone camera, no production, founder on screen or voiceover
over a screen recording. The only compounding Tier 1 channel.

## The format that works for this product

Not tips. **Narrative.** A coach scrolling past a "5 retention hacks" carousel
feels nothing. A coach who hears "she stopped replying on a Tuesday and I didn't
notice until the following Friday" sees a specific client's face.

Structure every video the same way:

1. **Hook (0 to 3s):** a sentence the coach has lived.
2. **Turn (3 to 12s):** the uncomfortable reframe.
3. **Payoff (12 to 25s):** what to do about it.
4. **Soft CTA (last 2s):** only sometimes. Not every video needs one.

Keep them under 30 seconds until something works. Caption everything, most of
this is watched on mute.

## Script 1: The quiet client (the flagship, post this first)

**Hook:** "Your quietest client is your next cancellation."

**Turn:** "Nobody quits because of a bad workout. They quit because they went
quiet for two weeks and nobody noticed. By the time the cancellation email
lands, they left mentally a month ago."

**Payoff:** "So stop opening your client list alphabetically. Open it by who
you haven't heard from. The client crushing it doesn't need you today. The one
who's gone silent does, and she's the one you're about to lose."

**CTA:** "That's the whole idea behind what I'm building. Link in bio."

**On screen text:** `THE QUIET ONE IS THE ONE YOU LOSE`

---

## Script 2: The 60% stat (highest save-rate potential)

**Hook:** "60% of clients who actually hit their goal still quit."

**Turn:** "Not the ones who failed. The ones who *succeeded*. They hit the
number, and then they left, because nothing came after the win."

**Payoff:** "Your best case study walks out the door because you treated the
goal as the finish line instead of the moment you're most likely to lose them.
The week your client hits their target is the week you need a plan for what's
next, ready to go, before they ask."

**CTA:** none. Let the stat sit.

**On screen text:** `60% OF WINNERS STILL LEAVE`

---

## Script 3: The spreadsheet confession

**Hook:** "Show me your client tracker and I'll tell you how many you're about
to lose."

**Turn:** "It's a spreadsheet, isn't it. Tab per client, colour coded, and a
WhatsApp thread you scroll up in to remember what you promised someone in March."

**Payoff:** "The spreadsheet isn't the problem. The problem is a spreadsheet
only tells you what you typed into it. It can't tell you who's gone quiet,
because silence doesn't get typed into anything."

**CTA:** "Building the thing that does. Link in bio."

**On screen text:** `A SPREADSHEET CAN'T SHOW YOU SILENCE`

---

## Script 4: The math nobody does

**Hook:** "Quick math on why you're always tired."

**Turn:** "Average client sticks around 3 to 6 months. You lose up to half your
book every year. So to stay flat at 20 clients, you're selling 10 new ones a
year. Just to stand still."

**Payoff:** "Which means every hour you spend on marketing is an hour you're
spending because retention leaked. Keeping one client an extra four months is
worth more than any funnel you'll build this year, and it's cheaper."

**CTA:** none.

**On screen text:** `SELLING 10 TO STAY AT 20`

---

## Script 5: The two-week window

**Hook:** "There's a two-week window where you can still save a client."

**Turn:** "Week one of silence, they're busy. Week two, they're embarrassed.
Week three, they've decided, and they're just waiting for the payment to fail so
they don't have to have the conversation."

**Payoff:** "Almost nobody reaches out in week one, because week one doesn't
look like a problem. That's exactly why it works. A message in week one is a
check-in. The same message in week three is a sales save, and they can tell the
difference."

**CTA:** "Building a dashboard that flags week one. Link in bio."

**On screen text:** `WEEK 1: BUSY / WEEK 2: EMBARRASSED / WEEK 3: GONE`

---

## Script 6: The honest anti-pitch (builds enormous trust)

**Hook:** "My app is useless to you if you train alone."

**Turn:** "I keep getting asked if it tracks PRs and syncs to my watch. It
doesn't, and it won't. It's not for lifters."

**Payoff:** "It's for the coach the lifter pays. One person: the independent
coach who builds the plans, runs the check-ins, and currently keeps it all
straight in a mess of spreadsheets and WhatsApp threads. If that's not you,
genuinely, scroll on."

**CTA:** "If it is you, link in bio."

**On screen text:** `NOT FOR LIFTERS`

**Why this one matters:** publicly turning away the larger audience is the single
most credible thing you can do. It also filters your inbound hard, which at beta
stage is worth more than volume.

## Script 7: Screen recording, the dashboard reveal

Voiceover over a real screen capture of the urgency-sorted dashboard.

**Hook:** "Every coaching app opens on a list of your clients. Here's why that's
backwards."

**Turn:** *(scroll a generic alphabetical list)* "This tells you nothing. Amy,
Ben, Chris. Who needs you? You have to work that out yourself, every single
morning."

**Payoff:** *(cut to the Logbook dashboard)* "This is the same roster sorted by
who's about to leave. At risk at the top. Check-in due. Waiting on your reply.
On track at the bottom, because they're fine and they don't need you today."

**CTA:** "Private beta, link in bio."

---

## Script 8: The plan-ended trap

**Hook:** "Your client just finished their 12-week plan. You have about four
days."

**Turn:** "That's the highest-churn moment in coaching and almost nobody treats
it like one. The plan ends, there's a gap, the gap becomes a week, and the week
becomes a cancellation."

**Payoff:** "The next plan should exist before the last one finishes. Not the
day after. Before."

**CTA:** none.

## Posting notes

- **Same asset to both platforms.** Do not make platform-specific cuts until one
  channel is clearly working.
- **Reply to every comment for the first two hours.** This is the entire
  distribution algorithm on both platforms.
- **Pin the best-performing comment** that asks "what's it called?"
- **Repost winners after 4 to 6 weeks.** Audience overlap is low and reach is
  fickle. A video that got 400 views can get 40,000 on the second try.
- **Bio link goes straight to logbook.fit.** Not a linktree. Every extra click is
  half your traffic.

- "The client who never complains is the one who leaves."
- "Nobody cancels a coach they spoke to yesterday."

- "Retention isn't a personality trait, it's a system, and yours is a spreadsheet."
- "Coaching is 20% programming and 80% noticing."
- "The refund email is never a surprise to the client. Only to you."
