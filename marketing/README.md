## The three facts everything is built on

1. The average personal training client relationship lasts **3 to 6 months**.
2. Trainers lose up to **50% of their clients annually**.
3. Around **60% of clients who achieved their goal left anyway**, because no
   follow-up plan was offered (PTDC).

Fact 3 is the sharpest. It reframes churn from a coaching failure (shameful,
unactionable) into a missing system (fixable, and we fix it).

## House style

- **No em dashes.** Use commas, colons, or periods. This matches the product copy.
- Sentence case in body copy, uppercase reserved for display headings.
- Be specific and concrete. Never claim a feature the product doesn't have.
- Turning away the wrong customer is a positioning asset. "Useless if you train
  alone" is doing real work.
