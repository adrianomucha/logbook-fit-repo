# First 30 Days

One page. If you do only what's on it, you'll have your first beta cohort.

**Target: 50 activated beta coaches.** Activated means signed up *and* invited at
least one client. Signups alone don't count.

**Daily commitment: about 90 minutes.** 45 on DMs, 30 on content, 15 on Reddit.
This is designed to survive alongside building the product, because it has to.

---

## Week 1: build the runway (no promotion yet)

The whole week is preparation and earning the right to post.

| Day | Do this |
|---|---|
| **Mon** | Build the prospect sheet. Source 100 coach handles from `#onlinecoach`, `#onlinepersonaltrainer`, `#strengthcoach`. Columns per `outreach-dms.md`. |
| **Tue** | Another 100 handles. Create the Reddit account if you don't have an established one. Read the rules of r/personaltraining and r/personaltrainers. |
| **Wed** | Film Reels 1, 2, 3 (the quiet client, the 60% stat, the spreadsheet confession). Film all three in one sitting, it takes 40 minutes. |
| **Thu** | Post Reel 1. Answer 3 Reddit threads, no links. Set up Email 0 so waitlist signups stop hitting a dead end. |
| **Fri** | Post Reel 2. Answer 3 more Reddit threads. Draft the TrueCoach alternatives page. |
| **Sat/Sun** | Post Reel 3. Light Reddit. Rest. |

**End of week 1 target:** 200 prospects sourced, 3 Reels live, 6 useful Reddit
comments, confirmation email working.

## Week 2: start the outreach engine

| Day | Do this |
|---|---|
| **Mon** | 15 DMs (variant A). Answer 2 Reddit threads. |
| **Tue** | 15 DMs. Post Reel 4 (the math). Publish the TrueCoach alternatives page. |
| **Wed** | 15 DMs. Answer 2 Reddit threads. First LinkedIn long-form post. |
| **Thu** | 15 DMs. Post Reel 5 (the two-week window). Reply to every DM response same day. |
| **Fri** | 15 DMs. Post the X build-in-public thread. Review reply rate on variant A. |
| **Sat/Sun** | Reply to any DM replies. Draft the Trainerize page. |

**End of week 2 target:** 75 DMs sent, 15 replies, 5 to 8 beta conversations
started, 1 SEO page live.

If reply rate is under 10%, stop and rewrite the opener before sending more.
Sending 200 messages with a bad opener burns 200 prospects permanently.

## Week 4: talk to users, then double down

This is the week most founders skip and it's the one that decides whether any of
the rest matters.

| Day | Do this |
|---|---|
| **Mon** | 15 DMs. Send Email 5 (the nudge) to anyone who signed up but never invited a client. |
| **Tue** | 15 DMs. Post Reel 8 (plan-ended trap). Start the second SEO guide. |
| **Wed** | **No outreach.** Spend the day on the feedback you collected. Fix the single most common confusion. |
| **Thu** | 15 DMs. LinkedIn long-form #3. Post in the Facebook groups that allow it, on their promo day. |
| **Fri** | 15 DMs. Review the month: which channel produced actual activated coaches? |
| **Sat/Sun** | Write the month-one build-in-public recap with real numbers. |

**End of week 4 target:** 200 DMs cumulative, 20 to 30 activated coaches, and a
clear answer to "which channel works."

---

## The week 4 decision

At the end of the month, one channel will be obviously ahead. Everything in month
two goes there, and the losers get dropped without sentiment.

Look at **activated coaches per hour spent**, not raw signups:

- **DMs winning?** Go to 25/day and start hiring out the sourcing.
- **Reddit winning?** Become a genuine regular in both subs. Post the follow-up.
- **Video winning?** Go to daily. Everything else becomes secondary.
- **SEO showing early movement?** Keep publishing weekly, but it won't be the
  answer this month and shouldn't pretend to be.

**If nothing is working:** the problem is almost certainly not the channel. It's
either the message (check DM reply rate) or the product (check whether coaches
run a second check-in loop). Fix that before spending another hour on
distribution.
