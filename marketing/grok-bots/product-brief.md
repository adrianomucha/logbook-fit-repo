# Logbook.fit — product brief for research bots

Upload this file to every bot Workspace so Grok judges "fit" the same way you do.

## What it is

Logbook.fit is a **retention-first coaching platform for independent fitness coaches**: personal trainers, online coaches, strength & conditioning coaches, and nutrition coaches who also program training — anyone who manages their own roster of paying clients.

It does three things:

1. **Urgency-sorted dashboard.** Ranks a coach's clients by who needs attention *today*: going quiet, check-in due, awaiting a reply, on track. The coach opens the app and sees who to worry about first.
2. **Structured weekly check-in loop.** Coach sends a check-in, client rates effort and how their body feels, coach replies with feedback and plan adjustments. Replaces scattered WhatsApp threads.
3. **Plan builder + exercise library.** Multi-week workout plans, personal exercise library, live workout execution for the client with flags back to the coach.

Tagline: *Your quietest client is your next cancellation. Logbook.fit surfaces them first.*

## Who it is for (ICP)

- Independent / freelance personal trainers with their own client roster (in-gym, in-home, hybrid)
- Online and hybrid fitness coaches doing remote programming + check-ins
- Strength & conditioning, powerlifting, weightlifting, CrossFit-style coaches with private clients
- Nutrition / health coaches who also write training plans
- Owners of small gyms and private studios who coach clients themselves or run a few trainers

Typical pain: "I keep it all straight in a mess of spreadsheets and WhatsApp threads, and I notice a client is drifting two weeks too late."

## Who it is NOT for

- People who work out alone (solo lifters, gym members)
- People looking *for* a trainer
- Big-box gym chains, franchise HQs, enterprise fitness software buyers

## Fit score rubric (1–5) for a Facebook group

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 5 | Independent coaches with their own paying client roster, discussing client management, retention, check-ins, pricing, business |
| 4 | Coaches, but broader topics (programming, certifications, general shop talk) |
| 3 | Mixed: coaches and fitness enthusiasts |
| 2 | Mostly enthusiasts with a few coaches |
| 1 | Poor fit (clients, weight-loss challenges, workout buddies, non-US) |

## Geography

United States only. National US groups count. Groups for a US state or metro count. Groups for other countries do not. "Worldwide" groups count only when the US is clearly the dominant audience.
